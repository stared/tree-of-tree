title: Trust, tryst, truce
---

The same faith hardened into nouns of reliance: **\*traustą** gave Old Norse **traust**, English **trust** — and a lovers' **tryst**, *the place one waits trustingly*. Beside them grew the old verb **trow** *to believe*, German **Trost** *comfort*, and **truce** — originally a plural, *pledges*: a faith sworn between enemies.
