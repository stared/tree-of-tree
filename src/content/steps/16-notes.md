I am grateful to [Miroslav Šedivý](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eumiro/), a Czechoslovak polyglot I met at PiterPy 2018, who sparked a curiosity that Polish **zdrowy** *healthy* is from **drewno** *wood* and **drzewo** *tree*. A few weeks ago I saw [Odd Pride](https://www.instagram.com/astrid_lundberg/)'s [short on the tree of life](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/k-ZWNfg0w3Q), which prompted me to make [an explorable explanation](https://p.migdal.pl/blog/2024/05/science-games-explorable-explanations/), a medium close to my heart.

See [a discussion on r/dataisbeautiful](https://www.reddit.com/r/dataisbeautiful/comments/1uf8tqa/oc_tree_truth_druid_dryad_tar_and_dendrite_grew/), and other [Proto-Indo-European roots](https://p.migdal.pl/pie-roots/), including **\*weyd-** *to see, to know* and **\*kʷel-** *to turn*.
