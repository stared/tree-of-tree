title: "A Slavic tree and health"
---

Slavic **\*dervo** *tree* lives on as Polish **drzewo** and Ukrainian **де́рево [dérevo]**. And Polish **zdrowy**, Ukrainian **здоро́вий [zdoróvyj]** *healthy* likely grow from the same wood — at root *made of good wood*, *as strong as a tree*.
