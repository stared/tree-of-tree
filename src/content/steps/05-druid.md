title: Druids, the tree sages
---

The word **druid** comes from Celtic **druwid**, and splits into two roots. One we already know. The other, **\*weid-**, means *to know* — it's the root behind **wit**, **wisdom**, Polish **wiedza** (knowledge), even **wiedźma** (a witch).

So a druid is an oak-sage, a tree-knower — or, taking the strength-sense, a mighty sage.
