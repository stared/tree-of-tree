title: Oak, the prototypical tree
---

More than once the root narrowed from any tree to the mightiest one, the oak. In Greek that oak is **δρῦς [drŷs]**. Celtic did the same: **\*daru** → Old Irish **daur**, **dair**, Welsh **derw**, and the Irish **doire** *oak grove* still names the city of **Derry**.
