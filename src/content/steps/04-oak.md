title: Narrowed to the oak
---

More than once the root narrowed from any tree to the mightiest one, the oak. In Greek that oak is **δρῦς [drŷs]**, and its tree-nymphs, the **Dryades**, give us **dryad** — and the **hamadryad**, fated to die together with her own tree. Celtic did the same: **\*daru** → Old Irish **daur**, **dair**, Welsh **derw**, and the Irish **doire** *oak grove* still names the city of **Derry**.
