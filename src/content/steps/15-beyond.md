title: Not all PIE trees are related

Not all trees grow from the same root — see German **Baum** or English **beam**. And Spanish **árbol**: the **rb** might look like a shuffled **dr**, but it grows from a wholly separate root, **\*h₃erdʰ-** _upright, tall_.
