title: The old root
---

Every word in this canopy grew from a single Proto-Indo-European root, **\*deru-** / **\*dóru-**, spoken maybe 5,000–6,000 years ago. It meant two things at once: *to be firm, solid, steadfast* and, as a noun, *tree, wood*. Those two senses — firmness and wood — are the trunk from which everything else splits.
