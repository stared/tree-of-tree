title: The oldest witnesses
---

The same root surfaces across the whole family: Hittite **taru** on 4,000-year-old clay tablets; Tocharian **or** *wood* from the Tarim Basin; Armenian **տորգ [torg]** *wooden frame, loom*; and Sanskrit **dāru** *wood* — whose **devadāru** *divine tree* we borrowed as **deodar**, the Himalayan cedar.
