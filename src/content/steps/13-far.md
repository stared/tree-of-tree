title: Edges of the family
---

The same root surfaces across the whole family: Hittite **taru** on 4,000-year-old clay tablets; Tocharian **or** *wood* from the Tarim Basin; Albanian **dru**; Armenian **tram** *firm*; and Sanskrit **dāru** *wood* — whose **devadāru** *divine tree* we borrowed as **deodar**, the Himalayan cedar.
