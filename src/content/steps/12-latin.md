title: Dura lex(icon)
---

Latin **dūrus** *hard* looks like a perfect fit for a root meaning *firm*, and a long tradition files **endure**, **durable**, **duration**, **dour**, **duress**, **obdurate**, even **dura mater** here. But that very first step — from the tree-root to *hard* — is the disputed one.
