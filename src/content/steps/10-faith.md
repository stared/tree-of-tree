title: As firm as a tree
---

Here's the surprise the dictionaries confirm: **true** is a tree-word. From the same oblique stem **\*dréw-** as **tree**, Germanic **\*treuwaz** meant *firm, steadfast — as a tree*. From it grew **true** and **truth**; the **troth** you plight when you **betroth**; the old verb **trow**, *to believe*; and even a **truce**, a faith sworn between enemies. And from the very same word **\*traustą** hardened into **trust**, and German **Trost**, *comfort*.
