title: As firm as a tree
---

Here's the surprise the dictionaries confirm: **true** is a tree-word. From the same oblique stem **\*dréw-** as **tree**, Germanic **\*treuwaz** meant *firm, steadfast — as a tree*; German **treu** and Dutch **trouw** still mean *faithful*. Old English **trēowe** gave **true** — and, built straight on it, **truth**, with the **troth** you plight when you **betroth**.
