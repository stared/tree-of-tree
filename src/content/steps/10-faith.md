title: As firm as a tree
---

What does it mean to be **true**? At root, to be *as firm as a tree* — and the two share the stem **\*dréw-**. Germanic **\*treuwaz** meant *steadfast*, a sense German **treu** and Dutch **trouw** still carry as *faithful*. On the same root stand **truth**, **troth**, and the vow you make when you **betroth**.
