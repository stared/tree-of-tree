title: Druids
---

A **druid** is, at heart, a *knower*: the second element is **\*weid-**, *to know* — the root behind **wit**. The first element is the open part, and every reading rests on the same root. From **\*deru-** the tree it is an *oak-sage* or *tree-sage*; from its strength-sense, a *great, firm sage*. Pliny liked the oak; either way the druid stays inside the **\*deru-** / **\*drew-** complex.
