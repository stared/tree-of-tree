title: Tree-pitch
---

Render the resin of certain trees and you get **tar** — literally *the pitch of certain trees*, from the o-grade **\*derw-o-**. It gives English **tar** and German **Teer**: the same formation as Slavic **dervo** *tree* and Lithuanian **derva** *resin*.
