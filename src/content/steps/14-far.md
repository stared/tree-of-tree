title: The oldest witnesses
---

The same root surfaces right across the family, in its oldest witnesses. Hittite **taru** sits on 4,000-year-old clay tablets. Tocharian **or** *wood* turns up in the Tarim Basin. Armenian **տորգ [torg]** is a *wooden frame, loom*. And Sanskrit **dāru** *wood* gives **devadāru** *divine tree*, which we borrowed as **deodar**, the Himalayan cedar. Beside it, Old Persian **dāruv** lives on as Persian **dār** — *wood* turned *gallows*.
