title: The Tree of *tree*
---

The English word **tree** and dozens of unlikely relatives — **true**, **trust**, **trough**, **endure**, **druid**, **dryad**, **tar**, even Polish **zdrowy** *healthy* — all grew from one Proto-Indo-European root, **\*deru-**. Scroll to follow the branches; the tree on the right grows from the root upward, exactly like the thing it names.
