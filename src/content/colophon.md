title: About this tree
---

A few words you might expect here stay out. German **Baum** and English **beam** come from a different Proto-Germanic word, **\*baumaz**; Spanish **árbol**, through Latin **arbor**, sits on a separate root altogether, PIE **\*h₃erdʰ-** *upright, tall*, the same source as **arduus** — the Romance tree is named for its shape, not its substance. And where scholars themselves disagree, this tree shows the disagreement rather than hiding it: the Latin **dūrus** family, **druid** as *oak-knower*, the Slavic *good wood* reading, and Greek **déndron**'s odd reduplication are all drawn dashed. Click any of them to see who contests the link, and why.

By [Piotr Migdał](https://p.migdal.pl/). Source, data, and per-word references on [GitHub](https://github.com/stared/tree-of-tree).
