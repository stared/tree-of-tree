---
title: Beyond PIE tree
---

A few words you might expect here stay out. German **Baum** and English **beam** come from a different Proto-Germanic word, **\*baumaz**; Spanish **árbol**, through Latin **arbor**, sits on a separate root altogether, PIE **\*h₃erdʰ-** *upright, tall*, the same source as **arduus** — the Romance tree is named for its shape, not its substance.
