# References

Every claim in [*The Tree of "tree"*](https://stared.github.io/tree-of-tree/) is
traced to a source below. **Secure** descent (agreed across sources) and
**disputed** links (dashed in the visualization) are kept distinct; this file
records who disputes what. Source IDs `[Rn]` match the `refs: [...]` arrays in
[`src/data/etymology.ts`](src/data/etymology.ts).

The root is written **`*deru- / *dóru-`** to hold two conventions side by side
— not a real disagreement, just different headwords:

- **etymonline** files the root **`*deru-`** "be firm, solid, steadfast," with
  specialised "wood, tree" and suffixed variants `*drew-o-`, `*dru-`,
  `*dru-ko-`, `*derw-`, `*dreu-`.
- **Wiktionary** files the noun **`*dóru`** "tree, wood, spear," an athematic
  proterokinetic noun with oblique stem `*drew-` and zero-grade `*dru-`,
  noted as possibly de-adjectival from `*deru-/*drew-` "hard, firm."

Pokorny and Watkins treat tree ↔ firm as one root; EIEC leans unified; NIL/LIV
tentatively split a verb *der- "to fix, make firm." This project shows them as
one family, branching by the noun's ablaut stems (nom. `*dóru`, obl. `*dréw-`,
zero-grade `*dru-`, o-grade `*derw-o-`).

### Root

- ***deru- / *dóru-*** "be firm, solid, steadfast" with specialised "wood, tree" — `[R2]`, `[R1]` — "to be firm, solid, steadfast… with specialised senses 'wood,' 'tree'"; *"*dóru* (neuter): tree."
- The noun *dóru* may be deadjectival from *deru-/*drew- "hard, firm, solid" — `[R1]`.

- **true** ← OE *trīewe/trēowe* ← PGmc *treuwaz / triwwiz* "faithful" ← *deru-* — `[R5]`, `[R6]`. Cognates German *treu*, Dutch *getrouw*, ON *tryggr*, Gothic *triggws* — `[R5]`. Semantic development "firm as an oak" → "firm, strong" → "true" — `[R74]` (Matasović EDPC 91).
- **truth** ← OE *trēowþ* (= *true* + *-iþō*) — `[R7]`.

## Disputed links

Each link shown dashed in the visualization, with both sides and the project's
current treatment.

### Latin *dūrus* "hard" ← *deru-*

- **For** (Watkins / AHD; etymonline): *dūrus* and its family (*endure, durable, dour, obdurate, duress, dura mater*…) from a suffixed variant of *deru-* "be firm, solid" — `[R24]`, `[R2]`, `[R25]`.
- **Against** (de Vaan EDL 184; Wiktionary): preferred etymology PIE *du(e)h₂-ró-s* "long, far" (cf. Skt *dūrá* "far," Gk *dērós* "long"); the *deru-* link demoted to an alternative — `[R26]`, `[R27]` — "there are semantic problems if the change 'long' > 'enduring' is not accepted."
- **In the tree:** kept under *deru-*, dashed. etymonline and AHD share the Watkins framework, so they are one tradition, not two independent confirmations.

### druid = oak-knower

- **For** (traditional; Pliny): first element from *dóru* "oak"; "they who know the oak" — `[R48]` (hedged "perhaps"), `[R50]`.
- **Against / nuanced** (Matasović EDPC 107; Wiktionary): the literal "oak" gloss is folk-etymology, but the first element still lies in the *derw-/*dru-* complex ("oak / strong, firm") — `[R49]`, `[R74]` — *druwid-* is "the priest with strong insight."
- **In the tree:** link to root is secure; only the precise gloss is nuanced. Shown dashed at the gloss level, not the descent.

### Slavic *sъdorvъ* = "good wood"

- **For** (the "tree/wood" reading): PSlav *sъdorvъ* = *sъ-* "good" + *-dorv-* (← *dóru* "tree, wood") → "of sound wood" → "healthy" — `[R63]`.
- **Against** (Meillet; Derksen EDSIL 478): second element from *dʰer-* "to support, hold" (PIE *h₁su-dʰor-wo-*), needed to satisfy Winter's law; "Derksen favouring" the *dʰer-* reading — `[R63]`, `[R70]`. Derksen softens toward "good wood" in EDBIL 2015 §434 after Petit 2004 — `[R71]`.
- **In the tree:** kept under *dóru*, dashed. The "of good wood" reading is the minority view.

### δένδρον "tree" ← *dóru*

- The reduplication *der-drew-om* → *déndron* is "highly atypical, so the formation must be regarded as uncertain"; Janda's alternative (*dem-* "house" + *drew-*) has a "weak" semantic basis — `[R42]`, `[R69]` (Beekes EDG 315). The English derivatives (*dendrite, rhododendron, philodendron, dendro-*) inherit the uncertainty at the deepest step only.
- **In the tree:** kept under *dóru*, dashed at the *déndron* node.

### *trim* ← OE *trymman* ← *trumaz*

- "The word is missing in Middle English after about 1250, which makes connection uncertain" — `[R15]`. The OE → PIE chain (*trum* "firm" ← *drumos* ← *deru-*) is itself fine — `[R16]`; the doubt is whether modern *trim* continues OE *trymman*.
- **In the tree:** kept, dashed at the *trim* node.

## Excluded — and why

Things checked and intentionally left out, so they aren't silently asserted.

- **Old Armenian *tram* "firm".** Not in Martirosyan's EDAIL — `[R75]`; the Wiktionary entry `[R66]` was an artifact. The genuine Armenian reflexes kept are *torg* "wooden frame" and *tarr* "element" — `[R75]`.
- **Armenian *caṙ* (ծառ) "tree".** Not in the *dóru* descendants list `[R1]` — a different root.
- **Latvian *darva*, Old Persian *dāru-*, modern Persian reflex, Hittite *taru-* "hard".** Not confirmed in the consulted sources. Lithuanian *derva* `[R64]` is kept as the Baltic representative.
- **Greek *Dryope*, *δροῦς*.** No Wiktionary entries (404).
